About Me

Hi, I'm Jianqi — a Product Designer working on enterprise platforms where AI, complex data, and human judgment intersect.

I design large-scale systems for financial institutions and regulated industries: banking, insurance, and logistics organizations where a workflow touches hundreds of people, thousands of documents, and rules that can't be bent. Over ten years across fintech and enterprise AI platforms, my work has centered on turning highly technical, ambiguous problems into product capabilities people can actually use. Right now that means designing how business stakeholders understand the performance and cost of automated and agentic AI processes — work that sits somewhere between interface design and deciding what a system should measure in the first place.

Much of my work focuses on building systems that grow well. I see workflows as living structures — they expand, evolve, and often gain responsibilities over time. Good system design anticipates this evolution. It creates patterns that future teams can extend, not rebuild, and leaves room in the architecture for capabilities we haven't imagined yet. My goal is always to make the next feature easier, not harder.

"When perspectives diverge, it's rarely about right or wrong — it's a signal that the model needs refinement."

I work with a low-ego, evidence-driven mindset. Enterprise products are shaped by people with very different expertise — engineering, product, operations, compliance — and when they disagree, it usually means the problem isn't framed correctly yet. On a recent project, internal experts pushed back hard on a direction I'd designed and validated. They were right. I took it back to the strategy team rather than defending the work, and what we shipped was better scoped for it.

And I care deeply about enterprise UX. People spend eight hours a day in these tools — far more time than they spend in any consumer app. "They'll get used to it" is not a justification for poor design. Thoughtful enterprise UX reduces cognitive load, supports better judgment, and makes teams feel more in control of their work.

Outside of work, I'm a museum-goer, a cat lover, and someone who enjoys slow weekends and good stories.
